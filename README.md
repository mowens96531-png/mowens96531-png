# Michael P. Owens

I build systems, then test them against reality. My work connects physical-world data, sensor hardware, and software.

## Building Strainwave

**Strainwave is my effort to build the force layer of the physical world:** make what objects carry measurable and usable by people and machines. My current bet is radar.

The product ambition is easily deployed measurement points on existing objects, with shared infrastructure reading many points. The business has to prove three things together: better economics, useful multi-point operation, and easy deployment. I want to change what is practical to measure and create valuable capabilities that were previously uneconomic.

Strainwave is in R&D. I’m establishing the technology’s capabilities before selecting the first market. The force-layer vision is the destination; performance and commercial claims have to earn their place through evidence.

## How I work

- Start with the customer decision and the binding constraint.
- Use first principles to challenge requirements and redesign around avoidable limits.
- Keep sourced facts, assumptions, simulations, and measurements distinct.
- Direct AI agents to build and challenge the work; independently verify decisive results.
- Compare against the customer’s best available alternative, including deployment and maintenance.
- Preserve failures and record what evidence would change the decision.

I’m interested in businesses where a useful capability becomes infrastructure through repeated use. Novelty is a starting point; customer value and dependable execution decide whether it matters.
